Groundwater Lithium & Dementia Prevalence in Texas
Merged 460,137 groundwater records with Medicare-derived dementia prevalence across 254 Texas counties. The full-sample result was null; I traced it to exposure misclassification and developed a homogeneity framework to correct for it, raising R² from 0.009 to 0.226.

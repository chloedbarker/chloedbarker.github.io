Capstone: Texas Water Quality & Dementia Prevalence

An end-to-end data science project combining environmental monitoring data with county-level health outcomes to explore potential associations across Texas.

Python SQL / MySQL Pandas / GeoPandas Data Engineering Modeling Geospatial Analytics

Problem

Environmental exposures and drinking/groundwater quality vary widely across Texas counties. This capstone investigates whether patterns in groundwater constituents (e.g., lithium and other parameters) align with county-level dementia prevalence measures—while documenting a reproducible pipeline and database design.

Data

  • Water quality: Texas Water Development Board (TWDB) groundwater / well datasets (parameter measurements over time).
  • Health outcome: County-level dementia prevalence (e.g., NORC Dementia DataHub / CDC-derived sources, depending on final dataset).
  • Geography: Texas county boundaries (FIPS-based joins; shapefiles for choropleths).

Key engineering constraint: consistent county identifiers (FIPS) across sources and a documented merge strategy.

Approach

  • Ingestion & cleaning: standardized dates, handled missingness, validated county naming/FIPS consistency.
  • Database design: relational schema for well metadata + parameter observations (ER diagram + SQL scripts).
  • Feature engineering: aggregated measurements by county/parameter/time windows; created map-ready tables.
  • Exploration: choropleths and summary statistics to identify spatial patterns and outliers.
  • Modeling: baseline regression / multivariate models (and extensions as appropriate) with clear evaluation.

Results (high-level)

  • Built a reproducible pipeline to merge environmental + health data at the county level.
  • Produced interactive maps and summary views to explore spatial variation of key water parameters.
  • Documented assumptions, limitations, and next steps for causal interpretation (correlation ≠ causation).

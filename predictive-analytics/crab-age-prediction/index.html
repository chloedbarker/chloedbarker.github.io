Crab Age Prediction

Predicting the age label in a Kaggle crab dataset using physical measurements and engineered features.

R Feature Engineering Linear Regression R Shiny Kaggle

The Problem

How well can recorded measurements predict the age label?

This regression exercise tests how well recorded dimensions and weights predict the competition age label. Some predictors, such as shucked and viscera weights, are not non-invasive measurements, so this is not a validated replacement for biological age assessment.

The Data

Physical measurements from a Kaggle competition dataset

Each crab has 8 recorded features: Sex, Length, Diameter, Height, Weight, Shucked Weight, Viscera Weight, and Shell Weight. The target is Age, measured in months.

8 Physical measurements used as predictors
3 Sex categories: Male, Female, Indeterminate
Age Target variable, in months

The dataset is close to evenly split across the three sex categories.

The Approach

Engineered features did more work than any single measurement

I explored constructed size measures, polynomial terms, and size-by-sex interactions to represent relationships beyond a single linear predictor. Their value is specific to the reported model comparison.

Results

A moderate, honest fit

The final model reached an R² of 0.702 and a mean absolute error of 1.14 months, summarizing error against the dataset labels in the reported evaluation.

0.702 R², final model
1.14 Mean absolute error, in months

No single measurement stood out: correlations with Age topped out around 0.65, which is why feature engineering mattered more than any one variable.

Key Insights

What the data actually showed

01

"Indeterminate" crabs skew younger

Crabs labeled Indeterminate had a median age of about 7 months, noticeably younger than the roughly 10 to 11 month median for Male and Female crabs, a descriptive association in this dataset; the label alone does not establish a biological explanation.

02

Sex mattered less than size

Male and Female age distributions were close to each other. Body size measurements carried more of the predictive signal than sex alone did.

Age by sex: Female (median ~11 months), Male (~10 months), Indeterminate (~7 months).

Model Limitations

Where the predictions still miss

Physical measurements are still a proxy. Evaluation used the competition labels, without independent biological validation. The reported error does not establish performance on other populations or validate a non-invasive age-assessment tool.
